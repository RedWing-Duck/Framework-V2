PLAYTEST QA — Week 01 Polish Build: THIS PLACE HAS A BIG FOOTPRINT
Build tested: big-footprint.html (polish build, validate(): 0 problems)
Method: three scripted playthroughs run headless on the real engine:
a first-time player, a chaos player, and a detail-oriented player
pushing the parser. Regression holds: both win routes and all five
endings work, and the clock values match the walkthrough.
"TOUR 1-16" = steps 1-16 of the verified walkthrough.

P2
==============================================================

ID: PT-01
Severity: P2
Location: "listen" with no noun, TOUR, R1 (and R2, R3)
Steps to reproduce: listen   (first command of the game)
Expected: an in-voice sound line (GDD 12.3: a known verb never gets
the confused line).
Actual: "You try that. The universe gives you a look usually reserved
for bad checks."
Cause: Script C2 says bare LISTEN uses the room's LISTEN line, and in
TOUR has no fallback. R1-R3 only have LISTEN lines on nouns.
Suggested fix: promote these noun lines to room lines, and add a TOUR
fallback.
R1 LISTEN: use X.R1.elevator LISTEN (TOUR / ESCAPE).
R2 LISTEN: use X.R2.marble LISTEN (TOUR / ESCAPE).
R3 LISTEN: use X.R3.cases LISTEN (TOUR / ESCAPE).
Global LISTEN fallback (TOUR): "Violins somewhere, footsteps on
marble, and the Don breathing through his nose."

ID: PT-05
Severity: P2
Location: ESCAPE, all rooms (readability)
Steps to reproduce: critical path steps 17-21, reading every reply
Expected: tension lines that land one at a time.
Actual: extra lines stack up. Entering R8 prints the room plus three
extra lines (checkpoint foreshadow, giraffe mark, clock band). A
wasted turn at the 6-turn mark prints three add-ons (turn feedback,
one-time warning, band line). On a stream overlay, the room text
scrolls away.
Suggested fix (rule, no new text):
- At most two extra lines per room print, in this priority: NPC or
checkpoint lines, then state marks (animals, Nando traces), then the
clock band line.
- On a turn where a one-time clock warning (6/3/1) prints, skip the
band line.
- Skip turn feedback when a warning or an ending prints the same turn.

1. Every info and Tier 1 verb with NO noun, in every room, both phases.

with and without an item.
3. Prepositional forms: "drop X in Y", "put X in Y", "look under X".
4. Nouns from OTHER rooms (must get the free not-here line).
5. Plurals and two-word phrases of every WORDS entry.
